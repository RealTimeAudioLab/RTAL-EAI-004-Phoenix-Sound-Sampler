Project Phoenix v0.7.23a - Quattro Keyboard Footer Fix

The QUATTRO KEYBOARD footer was shortened to avoid overlapping text on the OLED.

New compact footer:
  1-4 PLAY   5F 6M 7V   8 BACK

Meaning:
  F1-F4  Play sample slots 1-4
  F5     Filter
  F6     Mixer
  F7     Voice Allocation
  F8     Back

No other audio, MIDI, filter, mixer, or voice-allocation behavior was changed.
