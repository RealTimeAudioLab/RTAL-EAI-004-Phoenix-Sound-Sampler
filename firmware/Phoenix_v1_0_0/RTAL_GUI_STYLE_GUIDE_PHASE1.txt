RTAL Phoenix GUI Style Guide - Phase 1

1. Product title: PHOENIX SOUND SAMPLER.
2. Selection uses the Phoenix arrow; no inverted menu rows.
3. The right footer field is reserved for navigation, normally 8BACK.
4. Footer actions must be split across fields rather than combined until clipped.
5. The LEVEL meter uses three readable scale anchors: 8, 5 and 1.
6. Standard actions use consistent terms: PLAY, STOP, SELECT, BACK, EDIT, DONE.
7. New GUI work must preserve the fixed header, content and footer zones.
